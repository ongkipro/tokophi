# Billing & super-admin control (single plan)

> Moved **verbatim** from `AGENTS.md` on 2026-10-28 (Claude Code warns when `AGENTS.md` exceeds 150k chars, and it is
> loaded in full into every session). `AGENTS.md` keeps a short digest of the rules plus a pointer here; this file keeps
> the reasoning and the incident history. Words like "above"/"below"/"the next section" refer to the old single-file
> layout — follow the named file or section instead. **If this file and the code disagree, the code wins — fix this file.**

- **One plan "TokoΦ"**, all features. `@tokophi/lib/billing`: 3-day trial → **Rp50rb** first month → **Rp275rb/mo** (annual Rp2.7jt). Prices are the **DPP (ex-tax)**; whether PPN 11% is added is the **GLOBAL toggle `platform_finance.subscription_ppn_enabled`** (0101, Super Admin → Pengaturan → Keuangan, **default OFF at launch** — owner ruling 2026-08-24). All three invoice creators charge via `subscriptionCharge(base, ppnEnabled)` and store BOTH `amount_idr` + `tax_idr`; the faktur renders the STORED tax, never a derived one. Full money map: **`specs/docs/FINANCE.md`**. **Do NOT reintroduce plan tiers** (Pemula/Bisnis/Skala are legacy, unused).
- **Store lifecycle segments** (`storeSegment()`): `trial → first_month → regular → vip`. **VIP = `stores.comped`** — super-admin bypasses billing (free, always active, never billed; admin `getBilling` + billing cron honor `comped`). Segments drive the super-admin Toko catalog (4 tabs). ⚠️ **VIP is a BILLING bypass, not an abuse shield** — an operator suspension outranks it (below).
- 🔴 **AN OPERATOR SUSPENSION IS A SEPARATE FACT FROM BILLING — `stores.suspended_at` (0086).** The Suspend button did nothing for months and said it had worked: `setStoreStatus` wrote `stores.status` + an audit row reading *"Toko ditangguhkan oleh platform"*, but the lockout is decided by `computeBilling()`, which read only `comped`/`currentPeriodEnd`/`trialEndsAt` and **never `stores.status`**. The suspended merchant kept a fully writable panel (measured: a real product save persisted), and the nightly billing cron rewrote the column back within 24 h. **There was no working way to suspend a store.**
  - The two must **coexist**, which is why one column could not carry both: a store suspended for **abuse** that then pays must stay suspended; a store suspended for **non-payment** must reactivate on payment with no operator involved. `computeBilling` now returns **`status`** (effective — billing with the operator override on top), **`billingStatus`** (the subscription alone) and **`operatorSuspended`**.
  - **`billingStatus` is what the MONEY reads.** The cron's renewal-invoice branch and `getRiskQueue`'s lifecycle reasons both use it — keying them on the effective status would bill a paid-up store a month early for a lock that has nothing to do with money, and park every deliberately-locked store at severity 100 on the operator's worklist forever.
  - **`StoreBillingRow.suspendedAt` is REQUIRED, not optional, on purpose**: a read path that forgets to `select` it silently unsuspends every store, and there is no runtime symptom. `tsc` is the guard — it found a 4th read site (`getRiskQueue`) that a manual grep had missed.
  - **Never derive "did WE suspend this?" from the status badge.** `store.status === "ditangguhkan"` is *also* what an unpaid store reads past its grace period; deriving the button from it is what made "Aktifkan" flip a column, refresh, and come back reading "Aktifkan" again. Use `operatorSuspended`.
- 🔵 **MARKING AN INVOICE PAID BY HAND IS BOOKKEEPING. IT DOES NOT MOVE THE STORE'S SUBSCRIPTION — that is the RULING, not an oversight** (owner, 2026-08-20). `setInvoiceStatus` writes `invoices.status` and nothing else; there are exactly **three** writers of `stores.current_period_end` (`api/public/pay/callback`, `compStore`, `extendTrial`) and it is none of them. The action's own comment used to claim the opposite ("extends a store's paid period"), so the belief lived in the code, in the audit trail and on the operator's screen while the merchant's panel stayed read-only — and the operator's next move was "Gratiskan 1 bulan", which files a **giveaway** for a month that was actually paid for. **Entitlement moves on PAYMENT EVENTS, never on a ledger correction.**
  - The asymmetry follows from the same ruling and is not a gap to "finish": walking a paid invoice back to `gagal` does **not** claw back a period the callback already granted. Granting time is what "Gratiskan 1 bulan" is for; taking it is what a suspension is for.
  - **Both sides of the click say so** — the row menu before it, the toast after it (naming "Gratiskan 1 bulan" as the thing that *does* grant time), and the `platform_activity` line, which is read long afterwards by someone reconstructing what happened to a store. Pinned by `apps/super-admin/lib/invoice-flip-is-bookkeeping.test.ts`, whose three claim families were each proved red against their own mutation. **If this ruling is ever reversed, that test is what has to be rewritten — deliberately.**
  - **Lifting restores the BILLING state and nothing more.** A store that was also overdue stays read-only, and both the toast and the audit line say so rather than announcing a reactivation that did not happen. Granting time is what "Gratiskan 1 bulan" is for.
  - **The audit entry reports an OUTCOME.** `suspendStore`/`liftSuspension` re-read the row through `.returning()`, recompute the effective state, and log *that* + the operator's email; a store id matching nothing logs nothing. Same rule as `markLabelsPrinted` — report what you WROTE. `compStore`/`extendTrial`/`setStoreComped` no longer assert `status: "active"` either; `syncStoredStatus()` derives it.
  - Pinned by `packages/lib/src/billing.test.ts` → *"operator suspension survives computeBilling"*.
- 🔴 **A PAYOUT IS COMPLETED BY A COMPARE-AND-SWAP, AND `menunggu → selesai` USED TO BE LEGAL**
  (Task 5.10 / F-M15, 2026-08-22). `markPayoutPaid` read the row, refused `selesai` and `gagal`, and
  updated whatever was left. Measured against real Postgres with the shipped statements: a payout in
  `menunggu` — one **no operator had started transferring** — was stamped LUNAS with a `paid_at`,
  the merchant is told on `/saldo` that their money has been sent, and `processPayout` afterwards
  matches **0 rows**, so the initiation step is not skipped but made *unreachable*, with the audit
  trail recording a transfer nobody made.
  - The decision is now `update payouts set status='selesai', paid_at=now() where id=$1 and
    status='diproses' returning …` in **`payout-transition.ts`** — one statement decides and writes,
    so two operators clicking "Tandai lunas" cannot both pass a check (measured: exactly one wins).
    Zero rows ⇒ nothing written; a **locked** read then names one of four typed conflicts, and that
    read is for the MESSAGE only — turning it back into the decision restores the race.
  - **The action keeps the gate, the audit line and the revalidate, and writes no status of its
    own** — two writers is two rules. **Nothing is posted to the ledger on any path** (the balance
    was debited when the merchant REQUESTED the payout), which is what makes a repeated click
    non-mutating rather than merely harmless-looking. It lives outside `actions.ts` for the same
    reason `apps/admin/lib/payout.ts` does: `"use server"` + `@tokophi/db` at module scope cannot be
    driven by a test. Pinned by `apps/super-admin/lib/payout-transition.test.ts` (all four source
    statuses, real Postgres; six mutations, six reds).
- **Super-admin per-store control** (`apps/super-admin/lib/actions.ts`): comp 1 month, extend trial, VIP toggle, reset owner/user password (scrypt), manage store users, `suspendStore`/`liftSuspension` — every action audit-logged to `platform_activity` (with `store_id` + `admin` kind). **A suspension REQUIRES a reason** (≥5 chars): the merchant is shown it, and one nobody wrote down cannot be reviewed, appealed or lifted with confidence. The store-list row menu therefore only offers *lift* inline — "Tangguhkan…" links to the detail, where the reason can actually be collected.
- **`pct()` is a DELTA formatter; `share()` is the one for a part of a whole** (`@tokophi/lib/format`). The super-admin "Status Toko" card fed `count/total*100` to `pct()`, so the control plane read *"Ditangguhkan 5 **+83,3%**"* — a plus sign implying suspensions were climbing when nothing had moved. Two comments in `format.ts` already told callers to "format locally"; two obeyed by hand (`return-summary.tsx`, `keranjang-client.tsx`) and the third borrowed the delta formatter. A rule re-obeyed by hand at every call site is how the third one happened. ⚠️ **That card's slices must SUM TO EVERY STORE** — VIP was missing from `getStatusDistribution`, so it totalled 6 while the KPI beside it read "Toko aktif 2 / 7" and every share was against the short denominator. It has its own key type (`StatusSlice`, with `vip`), not `StoreStatus`.
- **Client "Akun" section** `/akun/{profil,keamanan,langganan}` (NOT under Pengaturan) — tabbed layout; avatar menu links here. Langganan has payment history + printable **`/faktur/[number]`**. Sidebar shows a subscription badge under the logo.
- **Payment channels are a PLATFORM decision** (ADR 0004) — super-admin owns `platform_payment_channels`; the storefront checkout reads *those*. The client-admin **"Pembayaran" tab was REMOVED (2026-07-13)**: it let a merchant pick channels + a fee bearer, but **nothing ever read either value** — the storefront read the platform list and no pricing code touched the bearer, so the UI silently lied. ⚠️ **`store_settings.payment_channels` / `payment_fee_bearer` are dead columns** (kept, not dropped — dropping is a destructive migration and super-admin may want per-store overrides later). **A merchant-facing fee-bearer feature does NOT exist** — the BUYER pays the fee, per the platform schedule. ⚠️ Since 2026-08-24 `@tokophi/lib/payment-fees` is **ENFORCED in checkout pricing** (COD 3% of omset+ongkir + PPN 11% on the fee · QRIS 0,7% of omset · VA BCA Rp6.500 / other banks Rp3.000 flat · e-wallet 3%), not display-only; `/api/public/order` is the boundary and each fee's `base` field is part of its identity. See `specs/docs/FINANCE.md`.
- ⚪ **`platform_payment_gateways.enabled` IS A RECORD, NOT A SWITCH — it gates nothing** (owner ruling, 2026-08-20). Checkout gates on `autolarisConfigured()` (the env key) ∧ `platform_payment_channels.enabled`; `/api/public/order` and `lib/actions/payments.ts` are the two places that decide, and neither reads this column. Its only consumer was an **"Aktif" badge** on the super-admin settings screen, driven by `autoGateway?.on` — so the control plane could show green over a PSP that cannot take a single rupiah, and go dark while payments kept flowing. There was no Switch for it either: a status light wired to nothing. **The badge is removed; the column is kept** (dropping is a destructive migration — same standing as `store_settings.payment_channels`). The channel counter beside it *is* the enforced fact and stays. ⚠️ A platform-wide PSP kill-switch does **not** exist: if one is wanted it must be ENFORCED at those two paths first — the way `platform_cod.enabled` is — and only then surfaced. Pinned by `apps/super-admin/lib/gateway-enabled-is-not-a-gate.test.ts`, proved red against the badge being put back.
