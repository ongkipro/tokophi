## What was actually missing, and what this change set delivers

1. **`apps/storefront/src/pages/kontak.astro`** — the contact page carried a STALE comment claiming
   "There is no `stores.email` column" (true when written, false since 0070) and rendered no email at
   all, even for a store that published one. Now: `const email = (store.email ?? "").trim() || null`
   from the Host-resolved tenant, an `Email` row with a plain `mailto:` link (same no-obfuscation rule
   as the footer) rendered **only** when set. Unset → the row is absent — no placeholder, no fallback.
   The hardcoded shared fallback this task feared was already removed in an earlier pass; what
   remained was the honest render of the real value.
2. **`apps/admin/lib/actions/store-profile.test.ts`** (new, real PostgreSQL on the derived `_test`
   DB, same harness as `purchases.test.ts` — the mocked tenant wrapper runs the real GUC +
   `set local role tokophi_app` transaction):
   - two stores persist different emails; each tenant's `getStoreProfile` reads back only its own;
   - under the tenant role, another store's row is invisible even to a raw `select` (0058);
   - an invalid address is rejected at the action (`{ok:false, error}`) and the row is untouched;
   - clearing stores `NULL`, survives a re-read, and comes back as `""` — never a placeholder.
3. **`apps/storefront/src/lib/kontak-email.test.ts`** (new, `test/source-code.ts` views chosen per
   assertion): the rendered email is derived from `store.email` behind a conditional (`codeOnly`),
   the `mailto:` href is template-built (`textWithoutComments`), and **no email-shaped literal exists
   in the emitted text** — with mutation self-checks proving both predicates fire on a regressed copy
   (the `public-guard.test.ts` rule: a guard must be proved red before it is trusted).
